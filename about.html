Who we are

Light engineered for lasting impression

AL SHOLA ALMODEA Lighting Co. L.L.C SP partners with architects, consultants, and developers to specify and supply high-performance luminaires—from refined interiors to demanding outdoor and industrial environments. We combine technical rigor with a curated product range so every scheme receives consistent quality and dependable support.

Our story

Founded with a clear focus on architectural lighting, we have grown into a regional reference for premium fixtures and professional service. Our team bridges specification, sourcing, and site coordination so projects move smoothly from concept to handover.

Whether it is a boutique residence, a corporate fit-out, or large-scale infrastructure, we align product selection with standards, budget, and design intent—without compromising on visual comfort or longevity.
